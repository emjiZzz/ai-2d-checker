# ADR-015 — Ground truth first: human markings are the basis, and measurement precedes learning

## Context

On 2026-09-07 the AI-mode corpus was deleted: `audit_feedback` (267 rows), `ground_truth_markings`
(212), `manual_check_sessions` (21), `audit_violations` (2,899) and `audit_sessions` (119), with the
learned bundle retired. The reason was structural rather than a matter of corpus size.
`AuditFeedbackDocument` requires `original_status` — "status generated by AI engine" — and all seven
correction verbs are overrides of a finding the engine produced. So `corrections` and `findings`
cannot contain something the engine never reported. A corpus built from them can measure how often
the engine is wrong about what it found, and can never measure what it missed.

A Manual Check marking is made with the engine disabled: `features.ts` forces every room in a
prototype build to a manual check, so `START COMPARISON` is unreachable and the engineer marks what
is on the sheet rather than adjudicating a finding. That store can contain a false negative. It is
the only store in this system that can.

The decision was taken as an owner's call and recorded only inside a work-log row. It has since
directed every week of work — 12 sheet pairs carry markings, 419 live, and three corpus pairs were
labelled from them — while the vault's stated direction still described grounding a Gemini finding
on an entity handle. This ADR is written to close that gap, not to change the decision.

## Decision

**Ground truth comes from human markings made with the engine switched off.** In order:

1. **The corpus of record is `ground_truth_markings`**, converted by
   `tools/eval_corpus.py from-manual-check` into label files under `tests/fixtures/eval/labels/`,
   reviewed by a person and installed with a named annotator. `audit_feedback` is no longer a
   ground-truth basis, for the reason above.
2. **Measurement precedes any learned component.** A learned head, a retrieval pass or a matcher
   may be built when there is a figure over human-labelled pairs it has to beat. Rung 1 under
   [[ADR-007 Re-scoping the Maturity Ladder]] is that figure.
3. **The two pools stay separate.** A dismissal and a marking answer different questions, and
   merging them lets "a human dismissed this" answer "is this on the sheet". `GROUND_TRUTH` is its
   own retrieval collection ([[ADR-012 Indexing Human Judgement as Retrieval Collections]]).
4. **A held-out pair must never have had a comparison run on it**, which makes prototype mode the
   only factory for one. Held-out pairs are exported before any run, not designated afterwards.

## Consequences

- **The learned verdict head is dormant, and its old corpus is not coming back.** It trained on
  `audit_feedback`; that store is empty and is no longer the basis. Whatever trains next trains on
  markings, and the 2026-08-19 bundle's numbers are history rather than a baseline.
- **Labelling has a route and it is not hand-annotation.** `from-manual-check`, then review, then
  `label --from`. The `worksheet` path stays as the fallback.
- **Stage 0b's held-out half becomes the binding constraint**, since labelling no longer is. Only
  sheets that have never been compared can close it.
- **[[ADR-014 The Ground Truth Verification Pass]] depends on this ADR** and stays `proposed`: it
  must be measured on held-out human pairs before it is switched on, and those pairs do not exist
  yet.
- **The July direction is superseded on the comparison path.** "Ground Gemini findings on CAD entity
  handles" is not the plan: there is no Gemini in the comparison at all
  ([[ADR-006 Removing the Three AI Comparison Methods]]), and the grounding was achieved
  deterministically through `EntityAddress`.
  Vector rendering (ADR-011) and DXF redline writeback are unaffected and remain landed work.
- **What this does not change:** [[ADR-010 Grounded LLM Summarization of Comparison Results]] keeps
  its narrow LLM role, since it composes prose about findings that already exist and never decides
  what changed. The deferred standards rules engine stays deferred.

## Alternatives rejected

**Keep `audit_feedback` as the basis and grow it.** Rejected on the structural argument above: it
cannot hold a false negative, and false negatives are the system's headline gap. Growing it faster
would have produced a larger corpus that answers the wrong question.

**Merge markings into the correction pools so one store serves both.** Rejected 2026-09-07 when
`GROUND_TRUTH` was added as a separate collection. A merged pool lets a dismissal be retrieved as
evidence about sheet content, and there is no training run in the retrieval path where that error
would show up.

**Wait for the learned head before measuring.** Rejected as the inversion this ADR is named for. A
learned component with no figure to beat cannot be shown to have helped, and this repo has already
paid for that once — see the ledger's negative results.

## What would reopen this

A measured result showing that corrections carry signal the markings do not. That is a real
possibility for precision — a dismissal says something true about a false positive — and this ADR
does not claim otherwise. It claims that recall cannot be measured from a store that only sees what
the engine already found.
